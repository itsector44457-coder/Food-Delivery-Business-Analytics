Title - 🍔 Food Delivery Business Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

Project Overview - This project presents an end-to-end Business Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI for a Food Delivery Application.
                   The dashboard analyzes sales performance, customer behavior, restaurant performance, delivery efficiency, payment methods, and operational KPIs to support data-driven business decisions.

Business Problem - Food delivery companies generate thousands of orders every day. Business managers need a centralized dashboard to monitor sales, customer trends, restaurant performance, delivery efficiency, and revenue in real time.
                   This dashboard helps identify business opportunities and operational issues using interactive visualizations.

Objectives - • Analyze Sales Performance
             • Track Orders
             • Monitor Customer Behaviour
             • Analyze Restaurant Performance
             • Evaluate Delivery Efficiency
             • Identify Revenue Trends
             • Improve Business Decision Making
